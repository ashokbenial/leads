Do & Don’t

Do

Keep the shield sacred

  • Always use the master Logo.png (or vector equivalent).
  • Keep clear space equal to the height of the “AB7” letters.
  • Place on clean, calm backgrounds (white, ink, brand blue or sky tint).
  • Use the knock‑out (white) variant on busy photography with a dark scrim.
Don’t

Never distort the system

  • Do not rotate, stretch, skew or outline the shield.
  • Do not re‑colour the shield outside this palette.
  • Do not rebuild the shield in a different typeface or shape.
  • Do not separate the wordmark from the shield for standalone use.

Usage Distribution

Across any composition the palette follows a 50 / 20 / 15 / 10 / 5 rhythm. Neutrals carry the page; Ink sets the stage; Blue identifies us; Green calls you to action.

50% · Neutrals & White 20% · Ink / Dark 15% · AB7 Blue 10% · Green 5% · Sky

Accessibility. AB7 Blue on White achieves 4.6:1 contrast (WCAG AA). AB7 Blue Dark on White reaches 9.2:1 (AAA). Signal Green on Ink 900 clears 7.5:1 (AAA). Never use Signal Green or AB7 Sky for body text on white — pair them with Ink or on dark surfaces.

06 · Visual Identity

Imagery & Iconography

AB7 imagery shows real, human work in progress — distributed teams, modern workstations, globally‑composed collaboration. Avoid stock clichés of handshakes, cogs, lightbulbs or glowing abstract tech.

Photography

Real, documentary, confident. Natural light. Warm skin tones. Crisp editorial crops. People visibly engaged in meaningful work (medical scribing, data annotation, customer operations).

Illustration

Editorial, geometric, restrained. Flat vector with AB7 Blue + Sky + one neutral. Use for concept spots where photography is unavailable or for abstract concepts like process or time.

Iconography

Line system. 24 px grid, 2 px stroke, rounded joins. Monochrome ink or white. Fill with AB7 Blue only for active or selected states.

07 · Expression

Voice & Tone

AB7 sounds like a trusted operator — direct, specific and a little impatient with filler. We are the advisor who has done this 300 times before and has the scars to prove it.

We are

Direct

We lead with the point: the outcome, the number, the timeline. We respect our reader’s time above everything.

We are not — evasive, vague or drowning in qualifiers.
We are

Specific

We use concrete proof: “60 minutes,” “60–70% savings,” “48‑hour onboarding,” not “fast” or “cost‑effective.”

We are not — breathless, superlative or jargon‑heavy.
We are

Warm & Human

We are a team of real people supporting real people. We credit professionals by name and tell stories about the work.

We are not — robotic, cold or corporate for its own sake.

Tone Shifts

Marketing / Site

Bold claims backed by numbers. Short, active sentences. One idea per line. Heavy eyebrows and data points.

Sales & Proposals

Structured and consultative. Open with the client’s problem, close with the measurable outcome and a 60‑minute next step.

Support & Operations

Calm, procedural, reassuring. Acknowledge, act, update. Always name a human who owns the next step.

11 · Applications

Usage & Governance

These guidelines are a living document. When in doubt, bias toward restraint: less blue, more white space, fewer words. If an application still doesn’t feel like AB7, pause and ask.

Approvals

External campaigns, press releases, investor decks and partnership co‑branding require review by the Brand Steward before distribution.

Brand Steward: director@ab7solutions.com

File Library

Store master assets (Logo.png, logo SVG/EPS, fonts, templates, brand photography) in the AB7 Solutions Branding drive. Never distribute rasterised screenshots of this portal as source assets.

Versioning

This portal is version 1.0 · April 2026. Material changes (palette, logo, naming) are versioned and changelogged. Minor adjustments (typography spacing, copy tweaks) are rolled silently.

Third‑Party & Partner Use

Partners, marketplaces and review platforms (Clutch, G2, GoodFirms, Trustpilot, Crunchbase, DesignRush) may use the primary shield with clear‑space preserved. No co‑branding inside the shield itself.
